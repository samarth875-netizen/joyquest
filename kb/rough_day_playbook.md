# Rough-Day Playbook (20 gentle ideas)

For low-energy, heavy, or overwhelming days. These are deliberately tiny — the goal
is not to "fix" the day, just to make it a little softer. No medical claims here, and
none of these is a substitute for professional care.

A gentle note: if you're in serious distress, please reach out to a trusted person or
a local helpline. In India, Tele-MANAS is 14416 (free, 24x7).

---

1. Stay in bed a little longer and let yourself rest without guilt.
2. Take one slow, deep breath — then another. Just three is enough to start.
3. Drink a full glass of water, slowly.
4. Open a window or step outside for a minute of fresh air.
5. Make the smallest comforting drink — tea, warm milk, or lemon water.
6. Put on one soft, comfy item of clothing.
7. Play one calming song and just listen to it.
8. Dim the lights or light a candle, and let the room feel softer.
9. Wrap yourself in a blanket for five minutes.
10. Put your phone face-down for ten minutes.
11. Write down one small thing that isn't terrible right now.
12. Sit with a pet, or watch a calming video of animals.
13. Do the tiniest tidy-up: clear one surface.
14. Stretch your arms and neck gently, wherever you are.
15. Look at one photo that makes you feel warm.
16. Text one person "hey" — nothing more is needed.
17. Eat one small thing, even if it's just a biscuit or fruit.
18. Lie down and rest your eyes for a few minutes.
19. Say one kind thing to yourself out loud, even if it feels odd.
20. Decide that today, doing less is completely allowed.

---

## How JoyQuest should use these

- On a rough day, suggest ONE small action, never a list that feels like work.
- Keep the tone warm and gentle. No pressure, no "cheer up" commands.
- If the user mentions serious distress, stop suggesting activities and gently
  encourage them to talk to a trusted person or a local helpline.
