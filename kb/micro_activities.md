# Micro-Activities (100)

A big list of small, specific things a person can actually do today. Each one is
tagged so JoyQuest can match it to the user's situation.

**Tags:**
- `time`: 10, 30, or 60 minutes
- `cost`: free (no money), low (small amount, roughly under ₹200), flexible (any budget)
- `mood`: which moods it fits best — great, okay, low, stressed, bored

These are general wellbeing ideas, doable in a city or town, and kept culturally
inclusive. They are not medical advice.

---
