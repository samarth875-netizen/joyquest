# Kindness Ideas (40 small acts)

Small acts of kindness are one of the most reliable ways to feel a little better —
for the person receiving them and for you. These are quick, low-cost, and doable
almost anywhere. Pick one and do it today.

A gentle note: these are general wellbeing ideas, not medical advice.

---

## For friends

1. Send a friend a voice note telling them one thing you like about them.
2. Share a song that reminds you of a good memory you had together.
3. Text a friend "thinking of you" with no other agenda.
4. Offer to help a friend with a task they've been putting off.
5. Save and send them a meme that made you laugh.
6. Recommend a book, show, or snack you think they'd love.
7. Ask a friend how they're really doing — then actually listen.
8. Remember something they mentioned before and follow up on it.
9. Cheer them on publicly when they share good news.
10. Make them a small handmade card or note.

## For family

11. Make tea or coffee for someone in your home without being asked.
12. Do one chore that isn't yours today.
13. Cook or serve a meal and clean up after.
14. Call a parent, grandparent, or sibling just to talk.
15. Ask an older relative to tell you a story from their past.
16. Help a younger sibling with homework or a project.
17. Leave a kind note somewhere they'll find it.
18. Thank a family member for something you usually take for granted.
19. Share a snack or your favorite dish with everyone at home.
20. Give someone a genuine compliment about their effort, not just results.

## For strangers and the world

21. Hold a door open and smile.
22. Let someone go ahead of you in a queue.
23. Give up your seat to someone who needs it more.
24. Help someone carry something heavy.
25. Give clear directions to someone who looks lost.
26. Thank a shopkeeper, delivery rider, or cleaner warmly.
27. Buy a small snack or water for someone who needs it.
28. Donate clothes or books you no longer use.
29. Pick up a piece of litter on your street.
30. Leave a positive review for a small local business.
31. Tip a service worker a little extra when you can.
32. Feed a street animal or leave out water for birds.
33. Share your umbrella or shade with someone in need.
34. Offer your auto or cab driver a bottle of water.
35. Let someone merge in traffic with a wave.
36. Help someone who's struggling with a machine, app, or form.
37. Return a lost item instead of walking past it.
38. Compliment a stranger's creativity or kindness out loud.
39. Send an encouraging message to someone who seems low.
40. Do a secret good deed and tell no one.

---

## How JoyQuest should use these

- Suggest one specific act that fits the user's time and budget.
- Prefer acts the user can do right now, close to home.
- Never pressure — kindness should feel light and fun.
